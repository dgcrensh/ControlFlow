# 2.5D roadmap: milestones 5 to 13

The milestones that take Character Studio from a vector editor with a rest-pose skeleton to a Live2D-style 2.5D
character modeler. The reasoning is in [2.5d-gap.md](2.5d-gap.md); this doc is the plan. Effort: S small, M
medium, L large, XL the size of several L milestones.

**Decided (2026-10-12):** Live2D-style (front-facing art that turns about ±30 degrees through keyforms
and deformers), vector only (Bézier points deform directly, no raster meshes), parameter preview in this app with
the animation timeline left to the separate animation system.

Each milestone ends in something usable on the iPad, as milestones 1 to 4 did. "Done when" lines are the
acceptance check; logic lands in CharacterCore with tests, per CLAUDE.md. Milestones 5 and 6 keep their numbers and
scope from the spec, with what 2.5D adds to them marked.

## 5. Binding and posing (L)

The spec's milestone 5, plus the rendering work it now needs first.

- **Deforming-path rendering plan (new, first).** Write `docs/plans/deformation-rendering.md`: measure the stress
  drawing with every point moving each frame, and choose between re-flattening on the CPU, skinning rest-pose
  triangles on the GPU with a re-tessellation threshold, or a mix. Rigid parts draw through a per-part affine on
  the GPU, extending what `LiveTransform` does for drags.
- **Pose mode:** drag a bone to rotate it (FK); children follow; the rest pose never changes; Reset returns to it.
  Setup and Pose are separate modes in the Bones tool.
- **Rigid binding** drawn posed: every part follows its bone, accessories follow their owner.
- **Two-bone weights:** auto-assigned by distance when a part is bound to two bones, then painted with the Pencil;
  saved as the per-point `weights` the format already reserves. Anchors and both handles skin.
- **Test poses:** save, name, apply and delete a few poses per character, saved in the reserved `poses` key.

**Done when:** the robot example can be posed by dragging bones; an arm bound to upper arm and forearm bends
smoothly at the elbow with auto weights and looks right after a little painting; posing the stress drawing holds
120 Hz on a recent iPad (or the plan doc records why not and what ships instead); two test poses survive save and
reopen; the example still re-saves byte for byte.

## 6. Variants and polish (M)

The spec's milestone 6, with variants per part (full-editor-slate #20) brought in, since slots and skins are the
same data.

- **Variants per part:** a part holds several drawings (mouth shapes, hands, eye blinks) with one active. Add
  Variant, a variant strip on the part, onion skin of the others, rename and reorder. Saved as extra attachments
  in the slot, which the format already allows.
- **Skins:** a skin switcher that refills slots (outfits as skins that fill accessory slots, as the accessories notes
  planned).
- **Sample characters:** at least two, one rigged and posed, used as test fixtures.

**Done when:** a mouth part holds three variants that switch in one tap and survive save and reopen; a second skin
swaps a hat accessory; undo stays usable with variants (bounded undo, performance audit #7, lands here if it hasn't).

## 7. Limbs: IK and rig cleanup (M)

- **Two-bone IK:** an IK target on a limb's end bone, with a bend direction; dragging the target in Pose mode
  solves the chain. Saved as a constraint on the bone (new optional key).
- **Rotation limits** per bone, honoured by FK and IK.
- **Mirror pose:** copy a pose from `.L` bones to `.R` bones.
- **Clean for rig** (full-editor-slate #22): a check that lists stray gaps, inconsistent winding and long segments
  with too few anchors to bend well, then fixes them on request.

**Done when:** dragging a hand target bends the arm at the elbow the right way; a limit stops a knee bending
backwards; mirroring a pose works on the robot; the clean-for-rig check finds and fixes seeded problems in tests.

## 8. Parameters and keyforms (XL)

The heart of 2.5D, and the format bump to 0.3.

- **Parameters** on the character: name, minimum, maximum, default. A parameter panel with a slider per parameter.
- **Keyforms on one parameter:** a shape stores point offsets (anchor and both handles) at chosen parameter values;
  the drawing between keys blends linearly. Keyform opacity too.
- **Edit at keyform:** set a slider to a key value and edits with Direct Select, Select (move, scale, rotate) and the
  style bar land in that keyform instead of the rest drawing. A banner shows which keyform is being edited.
- **Topology lock:** while a shape has keyforms, commands that add, remove or reorder points (insert, delete point,
  split, join, scissors, simplify) update every keyform of that shape, or are refused with a message where they
  can't.
- **Independent parameters combine additively** (eye open and mouth open at once).
- **Format 0.3:** `parameters` at the top level and `keyforms` on shapes, both optional; a migration step in
  `CharacterFormat.migrations`; schema, Schema/README and example updated together.
- **Undo** still one step per gesture; snapshot cost measured with keyforms present.

**Done when:** `EyeOpen` (0 to 1) can be defined, the closed eye drawn at 0 and the open eye at 1, and the slider
blends smoothly between them; `MouthOpen` works at the same time; inserting a point in a keyed shape keeps both
keyforms; a 0.2 file opens and saves as 0.3; the example re-saves byte for byte.

## 9. Head turns (L)

What makes the character look 2.5D rather than just animated.

- **Two-parameter grids:** keyforms on `AngleX` × `AngleY` (for example -30, 0, 30 on each), blended bilinearly.
- **Parametric draw order:** a keyform can override the slot order, so the far ear and back hair tuck behind the head
  as it turns; `behindSlot` and `behindOwner` still work at rest.
- **Parametric visibility and swaps:** a variant (from 6) can be chosen by parameter range, for features that change
  too much to morph, such as a nose seen side-on.
- **Keyform mirroring:** make the -30 keyform from the +30 one, using the `.L`/`.R` names and the symmetry axis.
- **Onion skin** of neighbouring keyforms while editing one.
- **Bone-driven parameters:** a bone's angle can drive a parameter, so posing the neck turns the head.

**Done when:** a sample character's head turns ±30 degrees left-right and up-down from the slider panel, with the far
ear tucking behind the head on the turn and the nose swapping to a side-on variant past a threshold; building the
left turn from the right takes one Mirror Keyform command plus touch-ups.

## 10. Deformers (L)

- **Warp deformer:** a lattice (3 × 3 to 5 × 5 control points) that bends every point and handle inside it; its
  control points take keyforms like shapes do.
- **Rotation deformer:** a pivot and angle that turns everything under it, keyable.
- **Deformer tree:** deformers nest; parts and accessories attach to a deformer or a bone. Shown in the Layers panel.
- **Format:** `deformers` optional in 0.3 (or 0.4 if 0.3 has shipped by then).

**Done when:** one warp deformer over the face, keyed on `AngleX`, shifts every facial feature for the turn, replacing
most per-shape keyforms from 9 on the sample character.

## 11. Turnaround aids (M-L)

Making head turns fast to author rather than possible.

- **Curved-surface helper:** wrap a warp deformer over a sphere or cylinder and seed the `AngleX` and `AngleY`
  keyforms from it, as a starting point for touch-ups.
- **Seed keyform from rest:** start a keyform as a copy of the rest drawing or a neighbouring key.
- **Reference per angle:** reference images tagged with a parameter value, shown when the slider is near it.

**Done when:** a new face can get believable ±30 degree turns in both axes in a few minutes of touch-ups after the
helper seeds them.

## 12. Physics (M)

- **Pendulum chains:** input from a parameter or a bone angle, output to a parameter or bone; length, stiffness,
  damping. Simulated in CharacterCore at a fixed step.
- **Preview:** physics runs while dragging sliders or bones, and can be paused.
- **Format:** `physics` optional.

**Done when:** turning the head with the slider makes the hair tips and an earring sway and settle.

## 13. Runtime and interchange (M-L)

- **Reference player** in CharacterCore: given a character, a pose and parameter values, produce the posed, deformed
  shapes. Headless, tested on Linux, and what the app itself draws from. The contract for the animation system.
- **Idle preview:** a simple looping preview (blink, breathe, sway) built from parameter curves, to check the model
  moves well. Not a timeline.
- **Spine JSON export** of the skeleton, rigid and weighted parts (vectors flattened to meshes), skins and poses, for
  playback in existing engines.
- **Format 1.0** once 13 is stable: from then only a major version is refused.

**Done when:** a test renders a sample character at several parameter values through the player and matches stored
results; the Spine export opens in a Spine runtime with the skeleton and a pose correct; the format is declared 1.0.

## Not in this roadmap

- A timeline with keys and curves (the separate animation system, per the spec).
- Raster art and textured meshes (decided: vector only).
- Full 360-degree turnarounds (can come later as variants swapped by parameter on top of 9).
- Face tracking or VTuber input.

## Risks

From [2.5d-gap.md](2.5d-gap.md#risks), mapped to milestones: rendering cost (5, measured before weights ship),
keyform topology (8), undo memory (6 and 8), byte-stable format across the 0.3 bump (8) and 1.0 (13).
